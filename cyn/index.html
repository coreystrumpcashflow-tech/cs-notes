    Speaker timer

    Runs on the stage monitor, so only the speaker sees it. The real time sits in the corner, and the timer changes color so they know when to land it.

    • Gold: 5 minutes left

    Worship backgrounds

    Tentative Fillers until the song list is set. Each song gets one pack: 3 looks on the LED wall, changed by hand when the song moves (verse, chorus, then the bridge or a tempo change). The 3 looks are the same scene with a small change in color and movement, so they flow into each other. The side TVs stay calm and dark under the lyrics. No words or logo on any of them.
